REPEAT STUDIO - SAMPLE AUDIO

The four short English phrases were written specifically for this demo.
Their audio was synthesized locally using the Flite SLT voice through
FFmpeg's flite filter. The sample is computer-generated speech, not a
recording of a tutor or a native-speaker pronunciation course.

Flite: Carnegie Mellon University, Language Technologies Institute.
Project: https://github.com/festvox/flite
License information: https://github.com/festvox/flite/blob/master/COPYING

No Flite or FFmpeg executable, source code, or voice model is distributed
in this package. Only the generated demonstration audio is embedded.

The application uses system fonts, browser APIs, and simple interface icons
drawn specifically for this project. It has no third-party runtime libraries.
