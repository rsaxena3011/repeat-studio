Repeat Studio · version 1.0

A little practice.
On repeat.

Turn a lesson recording into short listening and speaking exercises. Your audio stays in your browser.

Open the practice player

Your first practice, in a minute

  1. Extract the ZIP first. Keep this guide and Repeat-Studio.html in the same folder. Open the player in your desktop browser. There is no installation or account.
  2. Try the sample. Four short English phrases are already loaded. Press Start practice. The phrase plays, then a quiet gap gives you time to repeat it aloud.
  3. Hear your version. Press Record my voice, allow your microphone, and speak. Press Stop recording, then Compare to hear the original followed by your take.

Save before closing. Work stays in the current tab until you choose Save session. The downloaded .repeatstudio file contains your audio, phrases, settings, and the latest recording for each phrase.

Use your own audio

Choose Import audio, or drag a file into the player. MP3 and WAV are useful starting formats; M4A, OGG, AAC, FLAC, and WebM support depends on your browser. Use recordings you have permission to use. Protected streaming audio and video URLs cannot be imported.

Listen to the audio with the round play button. Drag A and B around a phrase, enter exact times, or use Set A / Set B at the current playhead. A phrase must be at least 0.25 seconds long. The + beside Phrases adds another phrase. Phrase names and text are editable.

You can import files up to 80 MB and 90 minutes. For audio over 15 minutes or 40 MB, the player uses a plain timeline to reduce memory use. Playback and A/B selection still work.

Set a comfortable rhythm

ControlWhat it does
SpeedPlays the original at 0.5×–1.5×. The browser is asked to preserve pitch.
RepeatsChoose 1, 3, 5, or 10 repeats, or keep going until you stop.
Match phraseMakes the speaking gap as long as the slowed or sped-up phrase, capped at 60 seconds.
Custom gapChoose 0.5–60 seconds of quiet speaking time.
Pause practicePauses during either listening or speaking. Resume continues the same repeat.
Listen oncePlays just the selected phrase without a speaking gap.

The final repeat also includes its full speaking gap. Practice pauses when the tab is hidden. Keep the player visible while practicing.

Recording and comparison

The speaking gaps do not record automatically. Recording starts only after you press the recording button and allow microphone access. Each phrase keeps one take, up to two minutes. A new successful take replaces the previous take. Download a take first if you want to keep more than one.

Compare plays the original at your selected practice speed, waits briefly, and plays your take at its original speed. The download button saves your take as WebM, M4A, or OGG, depending on your browser. Session files preserve that format.

Use headphones if helpful. Hiding the tab stops and saves an active recording. If microphone permission is taking too long, press Cancel mic request.

Save, move, and reopen

Choose Save session and keep the downloaded file. To continue later, open the player and choose the folder button, Open session. You can also drag the saved file into the player. Saving does not modify your original audio file.

Session files include any recordings you saved. They may be large, and they can be opened by anyone you share them with. A session made in one browser may contain a recording format another browser cannot play; use the original browser if that happens.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space Listen to / stop the phrase
P Start, pause, or resume practice
R Start / stop recording
[ Set phrase start · ] Set phrase end
Esc Stop playback or recording

Shortcuts are inactive while typing or using most controls. Focus an A/B handle and use the arrow keys for 0.1-second adjustments; hold Shift for 1-second steps.

If something does not work

  • The HTML opens as text or a preview. Extract the ZIP, then use your operating system’s “Open with” option to select a full desktop browser.
  • No microphone prompt. Open the player in a full browser tab. Check browser and operating-system microphone permissions. Recording requires HTTPS, localhost, or a browser that allows microphone use from a local HTML file.
  • The microphone is blocked or busy. Allow access in your browser and close other apps using the microphone, then try again.
  • An audio file will not open. Try a known-good MP3 or WAV. The player relies on the codecs supplied by your browser.
  • You cannot see a waveform. Long recordings deliberately use a plain timeline. If decoding the waveform fails, try the play button and numeric A/B controls.
  • Your session disappeared. Reopen your last downloaded .repeatstudio file. Unsaved work cannot be recovered after the tab is closed or the browser crashes.

The downloadable player is designed for desktop use. The layout adapts to small screens, but mobile file-opening, background audio, and recording behavior vary. This is a browser-based tool, not an installed mobile or desktop application.

Privacy and demo audio

The player makes no network requests for its operation. Imported audio, recordings, and saved-session processing stay in this browser. There is no analytics, cloud storage, or AI service.

The four English sample phrases were written for this demo and synthesized with a computer voice. They demonstrate the controls. For pronunciation practice, bring native-speaker recordings you have permission to use. See Audio-credits.txt for the synthesis credit.
