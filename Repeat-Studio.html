A LITTLE PRACTICE, ON REPEAT

Make the phrase your own.

  1. Bring your audio. Import a recording from your device, or try the sample lesson. Supported formats depend on your browser.
  2. Pick a short phrase. Drag A and B around it, type exact times, or play the audio and use Set A / Set B. Add a new phrase with the + button.
  3. Listen, then speak. Set your speed, number of repeats, and speaking gap. Start practice alternates the original with quiet time for you.
  4. Record and compare. Record my voice saves one take for this phrase. Compare plays the original, then your take. A new successful recording replaces the previous take.
  5. Keep your session. Save session downloads your audio, phrases, settings, and latest take for each phrase. Open that file to continue on another device.

Good to know

Work is held in this tab until you save a session. Keep the tab visible during practice. Headphones help when comparing recordings.

Each take can be up to 2 minutes. Long audio files may use a plain timeline instead of a waveform.

Microphone or audio trouble?

Allow microphone access in your browser. If the page is embedded in another app, open it in a full browser tab. For the offline download, open the HTML file in a desktop browser. Recording needs a secure context (HTTPS, localhost, or a supported local file) and a working microphone.

If a file does not play, try MP3 or WAV. DRM-protected files are unsupported. Browser and device support varies; use the sample lesson to check playback first.

Privacy and sample lesson

The player processes imported audio and recordings in your browser. It does not upload them, use analytics, or call AI services. Saved session files contain the audio and recordings you include; share them deliberately.

The original English sample is a short, computer-synthesized demonstration. Import native-speaker audio you have permission to use for your own pronunciation practice.
