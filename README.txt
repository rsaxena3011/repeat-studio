REPEAT STUDIO 1.0
Listen-and-repeat practice player

1. Extract this ZIP into a folder.
2. Open Start-here.html for the guide, or open Repeat-Studio.html directly
   in a full desktop browser.
3. Try Start practice on the included sample lesson.
4. Import your own audio when you are ready.
5. Choose Save session before closing to keep your work.

No installation, subscription, internet connection, or account is required
for the downloaded player. The microphone needs browser permission.

Included:
  Repeat-Studio.html   The complete self-contained player, with demo audio
  Start-here.html      Illustrated-by-controls quick-start and full guide
  README.txt          These instructions
  Audio-credits.txt   Synthetic sample audio credit

Supported workflow:
  - Import local audio up to 80 MB / 90 minutes.
  - Manually mark and name up to 100 phrases.
  - Listen at 0.5x to 1.5x, repeat, and insert a speaking gap.
  - Record one take per phrase (up to 2 minutes), compare, and download it.
  - Export and reopen a portable session with source audio and recordings.

Compatibility:
The player uses your browser's audio decoding and recording capabilities.
Audio formats and microphone availability vary by browser and device.
Designed for desktop use; mobile operation is not guaranteed.
Open the help button inside the player if you need troubleshooting steps.

Important behavior:
Speaking gaps do not record automatically. Work is kept in this tab until
you download a session. A new successful take replaces the phrase's prior
take. Save or download anything you want to keep before replacing it.

The app does not include transcription, translations, pronunciation scoring,
streaming-service downloads, automatic phrase detection, or cloud sync.

The included English audio is a synthetic demonstration of the controls.
Import recordings you have permission to use for your actual practice.
